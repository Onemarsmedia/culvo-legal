Privacy policy

The short version

Your photos never leave your iPhone. Culvo has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We don't collect your personal data.

Who we are

Culvo is made by Onemarsmedia Limited, a UK company. If you have a question about privacy, email hello@culvo.app.

Your photos

Culvo asks for access to your photo library so it can show you your photos and help you sort them. Everything happens on your iPhone, including picking the best shot in a burst, which uses Apple's on-device Vision technology. Your photos are never uploaded, copied to a server, or seen by us.

Deleting photos

Culvo never deletes a photo on its own. When you tap Delete, iOS shows its own confirmation, and deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days.

What's stored on your iPhone

To remember your progress, Culvo keeps a small file on your iPhone with the IDs of the photos you've already sorted, your toss pile, how much space you've freed, and how many free deletes you've used today. It doesn't contain your photos, and it's removed if you delete the app. Kept photos are added to a “Culvo – Ready to post” album in your own Photos library.

Sharing

If you share photos from Ready to post, iOS's own share sheet opens and you choose where they go. Culvo doesn't send them anywhere itself.

Subscriptions

Culvo Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details or Apple ID. Apple tells the app whether your subscription is active.

Children

Culvo isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any data from them (or anyone else).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date below.
