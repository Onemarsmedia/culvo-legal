Your photos, your call

Culvo helps you decide what to keep and what to delete, but you make every decision, and iOS asks you to confirm before anything is deleted. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. We're not responsible for photos you choose to delete, so please check before confirming.

Free plan

Swiping is free. On the free plan you can delete up to 50 photos a day.

Culvo Pro

Culvo Pro adds unlimited deletes, Bursts and Ready to post. It's available as an annual subscription (£24.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers) or a monthly subscription (£4.99 a month). Prices may differ in other countries and are shown before you buy. There is no weekly plan.

Payment and renewal

Payment is taken by Apple from your Apple ID when you confirm (or when the free trial ends). Subscriptions renew automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged.
