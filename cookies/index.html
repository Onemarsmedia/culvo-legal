Cookie policy

The app

The Culvo app uses no cookies, no advertising identifiers and no tracking of any kind. Small settings are stored on your iPhone only, as described in our privacy policy.

Legal and support pages

Our privacy, terms, refunds, cookie, accessibility and support pages set no cookies.

culvo.app

Our website is built with Framer, which counts visits in aggregate so we can see how many people visit. We don't use advertising or social media cookies, and we don't track you across other websites.

The waitlist form

The waitlist form is provided by Tally. Tally may store small files on your device to make the form work and to measure its own service; see Tally's privacy policy at tally.so. We only receive the details you type into the form.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. The app works the same whatever you choose.
