Accessibility

Our aim

We want everyone to be able to use Culvo, including people who use VoiceOver, larger text, a keyboard or reduced motion.

In the app

Every photo card has Keep and Toss actions, so you never need to swipe: with VoiceOver, swipe up or down on the card to choose an action. With a keyboard, use the right arrow to keep, the left arrow to toss and Command-Z to undo. Text follows your iPhone's text size setting, colours are chosen for strong contrast, and confetti and looping animations switch off when Reduce Motion is on.

On our pages

Our legal and support pages use plain HTML with headings, high-contrast text and visible keyboard focus, and work with screen readers and browser zoom.

Known limits

Photos in your library don't have descriptions, so VoiceOver describes each one by type and date. Very large text sizes may shorten some labels. We're working on both.

Tell us

If anything is hard to use, email hello@culvo.app and we'll do our best to fix it or help another way.
